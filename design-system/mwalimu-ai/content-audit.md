# Phase 2 content audit

Status: Phase 2 implemented on the `audit-redesign` feature branch. Phase 3 composition work is still pending approval.

## Changes made

- Replaced unverified homepage counts, ratings, county reach, testimonials, named team members, and outcome claims with descriptions of product areas that are visible in the repository.
- Replaced competitor-comparison copy with a product-area view covering learning, planning, and reflection.
- Removed claims about KICD alignment, 24/7 availability, guaranteed offline access, free-forever access, response-time commitments, and payment methods from general marketing and FAQ copy where the repository did not provide sufficient evidence.
- Replaced the blog's named author identities with the neutral `Mwalimu AI` byline because those identities were not verifiable in the repository.
- Removed the newsletter promise because the visible blog call to action led to account creation rather than a newsletter subscription.
- Kept contact/support form submission behavior unchanged while removing unverified phone, office, WhatsApp, and response-time claims.
- Kept authentication, OAuth, Convex, database, AI, Stripe, and routing behavior unchanged. Auth pages received copy-only changes; their forms and provider components were not changed.
